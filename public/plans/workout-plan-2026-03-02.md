# Week Overview

This week focuses on building upper body strength while maintaining cardiovascular endurance through Tuesday's soccer session. With an expected green recovery zone coming off a rest weekend, we'll push the intensity early in the week and taper into active recovery by Friday.

Given the lower back sensitivity noted in previous cycles, all heavy compound movements have been substituted with safer alternatives. Monday and Thursday serve as our primary strength days, while Wednesday offers a moderate-intensity conditioning session. The weekend provides flexibility for outdoor activities or additional mobility work.

WHOOP data from last week showed consistent HRV improvements, suggesting the current training load is well-tolerated. Let's maintain this trajectory while keeping RPE in check.

# Notes

- **Hydration target:** Minimum 3L water daily, increase to 4L on soccer and high-intensity days.
- **Sleep:** Prioritize 7–9 hours. Recovery scores consistently improve with consistent bed times.
- **Nutrition:** Increase protein intake on Monday and Thursday (strength days) — aim for 1.6g/kg bodyweight.
- **Lower back protocol:** If any session triggers lower back discomfort, immediately switch to the regression exercise and note it for next week's plan.
- **Progressive overload:** Aim to increase bench press and pull-up weight by 2.5kg from last week if recovery supports it.
