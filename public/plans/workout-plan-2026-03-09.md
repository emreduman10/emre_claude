# Week Overview

Week two continues the upper/lower split emphasis with progressive overload on key compounds. Based on last week's performance and WHOOP recovery trends, we're increasing bench press working weight by 2.5kg and adding an extra set to pull-ups.

This week introduces a dedicated conditioning circuit on Saturday to build work capacity. Tuesday remains locked in for soccer as always. Wednesday's lower body session adjusts intensity based on how Tuesday's match impacts recovery — check your morning WHOOP score before deciding on loads.

The overall volume is slightly higher than last week. If mid-week recovery dips into the yellow zone, scale Wednesday's session to 75% of prescribed loads and extend rest periods by 30 seconds.
