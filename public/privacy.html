Privacy Policy

Weekly Workout Planner — Effective March 8, 2026

Overview

Weekly Workout Planner ("the App") is a personal fitness application that displays weekly workout plans and integrates with WHOOP to show real-time recovery metrics. This policy describes what data the App accesses, how it is used, and your rights.

Data We Collect

When you sign in with your WHOOP account, the App accesses the following data from the WHOOP API:

  • Recovery metrics — recovery score, heart rate variability (HRV), and resting heart rate (RHR)
  • Cycle data — daily strain score
  • OAuth tokens — access and refresh tokens used to authenticate with the WHOOP API on your behalf

The App does not collect your name, email address, location, or any other personal information beyond what is listed above.

How We Use Your Data

Your WHOOP data is used solely to:

  • Display your current recovery score, HRV, resting heart rate, and strain within the App
  • Help inform your workout planning decisions based on your recovery status

Your data is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising purposes.

Data Storage & Retention

  • OAuth tokens are stored in server memory only and are cleared when the server restarts.
  • WHOOP metrics are fetched on demand and not stored persistently — they are displayed in real time and discarded.
  • No database or permanent storage is used for your health data.

Third-Party Services

The App connects to the WHOOP API using the OAuth 2.0 Authorization Code flow. When you sign in, you are redirected to WHOOP's own login page to grant access. The App never sees your WHOOP password. For details on how WHOOP handles your data, see WHOOP's Privacy Policy.

Your Rights

  • You can sign out at any time from within the App, which immediately clears your tokens from server memory.
  • You can revoke access to the App at any time through your WHOOP account settings.
  • You may request deletion of any data by contacting us at the email below.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
